A deployment-oriented evaluation framework for dense UAV canopy imagery, where robustness matters as much as raw accuracy.

What Is New In This Methodology

  • Annotation-free robustness scoring: replaces expensive full manual validation with four complementary consistency metrics.
  • Four-metric deployment lens: RCS (spatial stability), CSC (scale consistency), SVS (semantic-visual coherence), and ARI (repeatability).
  • Domain-aware evaluation: tailored for dense non-green ornamental canopies with shadow, occlusion, and color-shift challenges.
  • Actionable thresholds: production-ready guidance using RCS > 0.95, CSC > 0.90, SVS > 0.55, ARI > 0.85.
